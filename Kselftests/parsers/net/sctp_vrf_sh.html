parse_line

parse_line($string);

sctp_vrf.sh does not use net/lib.sh's log_test(); it prints its own numbered result on one line, e.g.:

# TEST 01: nobind, connect from client 1, l3mdev_accept=1, Y [PASS]

Lines that do not match this format are returned unchanged. A SKIP status is left untouched rather than converted (see net.pm).
